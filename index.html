Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the applications developed by Vertex One (represented by Oleksii Isaiev, PhD), including but not limited to:

  • Modern Furniture Mod for MCPE
  • EcoScanner
  • Chainsaw Man Mod, Diamond Portal Mod, Dragon Block Mod, Mod Wither Storm, One Piece Mod.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data within our applications.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our applications do not collect, store, or share any Personal Identification Information (PII) from our users. We do not have access to your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal data.

2. Permissions Required

  • Camera Access: Used by EcoScanner for real-time analysis and object scanning. No images or videos are stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Storage Access (Read/Write): Used by Minecraft Mods (including Modern Furniture Mod) solely to install mod files (.mcpack, .mcaddon) into the game directory on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide our services and monetize the apps, we use third-party tools that may collect technical data (like advertising IDs). You can find their privacy policies here:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

4. Children’s Privacy

Our services are safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with any data, please contact us, and we will take immediate action.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Oleksii Isaiev, PhD at your developer email address.
