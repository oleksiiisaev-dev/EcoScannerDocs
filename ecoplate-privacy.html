EcoPlate Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026. Applies to the EcoPlate: AI Calorie Counter app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

EcoPlate is developed by Vertex One ("we"), contact betterskills185@gmail.com. The app has no user accounts and no servers of our own. We do not sell your personal data, and your health information is never used for advertising.

1. Data kept only on your device

Your profile (goal, sex, age, height, weight, activity, food restrictions, training experience, the health conditions you choose to enter), food diary, weight history, water, fasting timer, shopping list, menu and training choices, reminders and language stay on your device. We do not receive them.

2. Health information and your consent

Health conditions you enter, and data from Apple Health, are sensitive. The app asks for your consent before using conditions you enter. It uses them only on your device, to make its advice more careful: for example to limit salt in the menu, hide the fasting timer or choose gentle exercises. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing them in Settings or deleting all data.

3. AI features (Google Gemini)

  • Features marked as AI are optional and need your agreement. Content is sent through Google Firebase to Google's Gemini AI: a compressed meal photo; meal text you type; products you list for a fridge recipe, with remaining calories and food restrictions; for an AI training plan, your age, sex, height, weight, goal, experience, training place, days per week and any special request you type.
  • Diagnoses you select in the app are never sent to the AI. The app turns them on your device into neutral rules such as "no jumping" or "keep salt low". Please don't type diagnoses, names or other personal details into text fields, or photograph people or documents.
  • Under Google's free service tier, Google may keep submitted content, use it to improve its products, and its staff may review it.
  • AI answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. They are approximate and are not medical advice.
  • Availability: AI features are currently switched off for people in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom. No content is sent to the AI from those regions.

4. Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode or ask for an online product search, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts. Any internet service can see your IP address. Product data is under the Open Database License (ODbL).

5. Videos

"Watch video" opens a YouTube search in an in-app browser. YouTube and Google handle that page under their own terms. We send nothing about you.

The app can also recommend our other app, EcoScanner, including in Premium. To show its App Store page, the app asks Apple's public App Store service for the listing, and Apple can see your IP address. Your diary, profile and health data are not sent, and we do not learn whether you installed it.

6. App integrity and remote settings

Firebase App Check (Apple App Attest) confirms requests come from the genuine app. Firebase Remote Config lets us change the AI model or switch AI off. These use technical device and app information, not your diary or health information.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The free version shows ads; Premium has none. Ad providers may collect your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information and ad interactions.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you tap "Allow" in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are also asked through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice in Settings.
  • Users who state an age under 18 are marked so that they do not get personalized ads.
  • Your diary, profile, conditions and Apple Health data are never passed to ad providers.

8. Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you turn this on, the app writes food, water, weight and workouts to Apple Health, and reads steps, active calories, weight and sleep to show them in the app. A workout started on Apple Watch measures heart rate and active calories. Today's calories and your next workout pass from your iPhone to your own Apple Watch and widget. Data read from Apple Health stays on your device: it is not sent to us or the AI, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

9. Notifications and purchases

Reminders are created on your device. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details, only whether a subscription is active.

10. Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent (including explicit consent for health data), on providing our service, and on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy and consumer health data laws such as those of California and Washington, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, withdraw consent, opt out of targeted advertising and complain to a supervisory authority, without being treated worse for doing so. Because your data stays on your device, you can use most of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, write to betterskills185@gmail.com.

11. Consumer health data notice

Categories: the health information described in sections 1, 2 and 8, and what you choose to send under section 3. Purpose: providing the app's features. It is kept on your device. Shared: only what you send to Google under section 3, to provide that feature. We do not sell consumer health data.

12. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you remove it. "Delete all my data" in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it from the device. This does not cancel a subscription. Content already sent to Google is handled under Google's terms. A count of used free AI photos stays until the app is deleted, to prevent abuse of the free limit.

13. Transfers and security

Google and Open Food Facts may process data in other countries under their own safeguards. Data on your device is protected by iOS and connections are encrypted, but no system is completely secure.

14. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe a child has used AI features, contact us.

15. Health notice

EcoPlate is a general wellness app, not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. See the Terms of Use.

16. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date. Translations are for convenience; if they differ, the English text applies unless the law of your country says otherwise. Questions: betterskills185@gmail.com.
